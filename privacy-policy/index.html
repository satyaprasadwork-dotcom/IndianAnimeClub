Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Indian Anime Club may receive, use and protect information when you visit the website or contact us. It should be read alongside the privacy notices of any third-party services you choose to use.

Website operator and contact

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused anime community founded by Satya Prasad. For privacy questions, contact prasad@indiananimeclub.com.

Information you provide

When you email us or contact us through social platforms, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your email address, username, message and attachments. We use that information to respond, review feedback, handle requests and maintain relevant correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless necessary.

Analytics and technical information

The site is configured to use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-M4738D1ZND) to understand general usage and improve the website. Depending on the configuration and your settings, analytics may process page views, device and browser information, approximate location, referral information and interactions. See Google's Privacy Policy and its explanation of how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies and advertising

Your browser and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. This policy does not claim that third-party advertising is currently being served. If advertising or additional tracking services are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe the relevant practices and choices.

Third-party links

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp, Google and other services take you to providers we do not control. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use their services.

Retention and security

We retain messages for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep relevant records, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. We take reasonable steps to limit access, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent information that should be removed, contact us.

Your choices and policy updates

You may choose not to contact us, manage cookies through your browser and use available controls offered by third-party services. You can ask about information you sent us or request deletion where appropriate by emailing prasad@indiananimeclub.com. We may update this policy as practices or requirements change.
