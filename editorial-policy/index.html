How we publish

Original thought. Clear sourcing. Honest context.

This page explains the standards we aim to follow when publishing information and commentary about anime culture in India.

Purpose and scope

Indian Anime Club focuses on anime fandom in India: community experiences, fan culture, creative work, industry context relevant to Indian audiences, and thoughtful discussion of anime as a medium. We aim to make content useful to readers rather than publish pages just to fill space.

Originality and added value

Published material should offer a clear purpose, original explanation, informed perspective, useful curation, or analysis. We do not intend to copy other sites and simply rephrase their work. Summaries should add context and point readers toward reliable primary or authoritative sources where possible.

Facts and opinions

We aim to distinguish verifiable facts from personal interpretation, recommendations, and opinion. Claims about releases, licensing, companies, creators, or community activity should be checked against credible sources before publication. If information is uncertain or has not been confirmed, we should say so.

Images, copyright, and attribution

We respect the intellectual property of anime studios, publishers, artists, photographers, and fans. We aim to use material we have permission to use or that is appropriately licensed, and to provide credit where required. Being a fan site does not automatically grant permission to reproduce copyrighted work.

Corrections and feedback

If you spot a factual error, broken link, missing attribution, or unclear statement, email prasad@indiananimeclub.com with the page URL and supporting detail. We will review reasonable correction requests and update material when warranted.

Commercial transparency

If we publish sponsored, affiliate, or otherwise commercially influenced material in the future, it should be clearly identified. We do not claim a partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, or event unless it is genuine and can be represented accurately.

Our commitment

These standards describe the approach we intend to follow. As the site develops, we will keep improving clarity, usefulness, accessibility, and accountability.
