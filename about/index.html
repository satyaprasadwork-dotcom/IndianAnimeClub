About Indian Anime Club

About Indian Anime Club

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused community for anime fans. We publish commentary, practical guides and perspectives on how anime is discovered, discussed and enjoyed across India.

Our purpose

Anime brings people together through memorable characters, imaginative worlds, music and stories. Indian Anime Club aims to give fans in India a thoughtful place to explore those stories and the culture around them, beyond rankings and hype.

Our focus

We cover fan culture in India, viewing and language preferences, creative participation, community experiences, and the wider anime ecosystem where it is relevant to Indian readers. We distinguish personal interpretation from factual claims and aim to make each page useful to its audience.

Our standards

We value original analysis, clear attribution, respectful discussion, accessible explanations and transparent corrections. We respect the creators and rights holders behind anime and manga and do not claim ownership of their intellectual property.

Community and activities

The website shares editorial content and ways to connect with fellow fans. Events or collaborations are only confirmed when explicitly announced as such. Please check our official channels for current updates rather than assuming that a proposed activity is scheduled.

Contact and accountability

Questions, corrections and feedback are welcome. Contact us through our Contact page. Our Editorial Policy explains our publishing standards, and our Community Guidelines outline expectations for respectful participation.
