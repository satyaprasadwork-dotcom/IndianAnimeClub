Why we exist

Anime is more than a trend. It is a part of our lives.

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused community for people who genuinely care about anime and the culture around it.

Our purpose

For many fans in India, anime was not always easy to find. We remember slow downloads, shared discs, recommendations passed between friends, and the excitement of discovering a story before it was part of every conversation. Indian Anime Club exists to honour that history while making room for the next generation of fans.

We want to build a community where anime is discussed with curiosity and context—not treated only as a trend, a product, or a way to sell something. Fans should be able to talk about the writing, animation, music, characters, themes, and the personal memories that make a series matter to them.

What makes this an Indian anime community?

Our focus is the experience of anime fans in India: how people here discover and enjoy anime, how fandom has grown, the communities fans build, Indian perspectives on anime culture, and the creators and initiatives contributing to that local ecosystem. We respect that anime comes from many artists and studios, and we do not claim ownership of their work.

What we want to build

  • Real conversation: thoughtful discussions, recommendations, and respectful disagreement.
  • Fan creativity: space to celebrate original fan art, cosplay, writing, and other community-made work with proper credit.
  • Accessible participation: a welcoming place for new viewers and long-time fans, across cities and backgrounds.
  • Useful information: clear, honest content that distinguishes personal opinion from verified facts.
  • Trust: transparent communication and no pretending that plans, partnerships, or activities exist when they do not.
Our promise: Indian Anime Club is being built around the people who love anime in India—not around hype for its own sake. We will be clear about what is active, what is planned, and what is simply an idea.

Is the club hosting events right now?

There are no public events listed at this time. We will share confirmed community activities through our official channels when there is something concrete to announce. Please do not treat ideas or general community interests as confirmed bookings.
