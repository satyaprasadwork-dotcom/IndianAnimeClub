# Indian Anime Club

**Website:** https://indiananimeclub.com/  
**Founder:** Satya Prasad

## Our goal

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-rooted community for people who care about anime beyond the latest trend. We want to create a welcoming space for anime fans in India to share perspectives, talk about storytelling and creative craft, celebrate fan creativity, and connect through a genuine respect for the medium.

The club is shaped by the experience of discovering anime before it was easy to find: downloading episodes over modem connections, watching DVDs, and sharing series with friends. Founder Satya Prasad has watched anime for more than 20 years. That history informs the club's belief that fandom is built through curiosity, shared memories, and people—not just social media hype or commercial promotion.

## Principles

- **India-focused:** centre the experiences, culture, and perspectives of anime fans in India.
- **Fan-first:** value community, thoughtful discussion, and creativity over hype.
- **Original and useful:** publish content with a clear purpose, context, and genuine added value.
- **Honest:** do not invent events, partnerships, team members, or claims of activity.
- **Respect creators:** respect copyright, attribution, and the work of anime studios, publishers, artists, and fans.
- **Welcoming:** support fans across experience levels and preferences without gatekeeping.
- **Privacy-aware:** explain analytics and data practices clearly and link to privacy information.

## Website structure

- `index.html` — home page and overview of the club.

- `privacy-policy.html` — analytics, contact information, third-party services, and privacy choices.
- `terms.html` — website terms of use.
- `disclaimer.html` — independence, intellectual property, opinions, and external links.

- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `sitemap_index.xml` — crawl guidance and sitemap discovery.

## Content and maintenance

- Do not add speculative event dates or present ideas as confirmed activities. There is no public event schedule at this time.
- Keep founder and leadership information factual; list additional leaders only after they formally join and agree to be represented.
- Prefer original explanations, informed commentary, and transparent sourcing over copied or lightly rewritten material.
- Review broken links, mobile navigation, metadata, accessibility, and sitemap entries whenever pages change.
- Privacy and legal pages are general website disclosures, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Indian Anime Club exists to build trust with India's anime community, one useful and honest interaction at a time.
