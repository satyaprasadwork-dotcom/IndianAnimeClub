Independent status and affiliation

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused anime fan community. Unless a specific relationship is expressly and accurately disclosed, the website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to anime studios, manga publishers, animation workers, distributors, streaming services, event organisers, or other rights holders.

Names, trademarks, and copyrighted works

Anime and manga titles, characters, logos, music, artwork, and other creative materials belong to their respective owners. References may be included to identify a work, discuss a story, explain a practical issue, or provide fan-community context. A reference does not mean Indian Anime Club owns that material or has permission for every possible reuse.

Readers who create or publish fan art, edits, cosplay photography, videos, or other works should check the rules and permissions that apply to their particular use. Crediting a creator is important but does not automatically replace permission where it is required.

Opinions and interpretation

Story analysis, rankings, reviews, recommendations, and cultural interpretations may reflect the author's judgment. Different viewers can reach different conclusions from the same scene. We aim to explain the reasoning behind an interpretation rather than present a subjective view as the only correct answer.

Accuracy and changing information

We aim to check factual claims and correct material errors when they are identified, but cannot guarantee that every detail is complete, current, or free from mistakes. Release dates, licensing, streaming availability, prices, product listings, and platform features can change. Confirm time-sensitive information with the relevant official source before acting on it.

Events and partnerships

Unless a specific activity is clearly marked as confirmed, a general idea, conversation, or invitation to discuss a possible activity should not be interpreted as a booking, ticket sale, endorsement, partnership, or commitment. This website currently does not publish a public event schedule.

External links

We link to external pages to help readers find original or additional information. Those sites are controlled by their own operators and may change, move, or become unavailable. Indian Anime Club does not control their content, terms, privacy practices, or security.

No professional advice

Articles and community pages are published for general information, discussion, and fan education. They are not legal, financial, medical, or other professional advice. Where a decision has important legal or financial consequences, consult a suitably qualified professional who can consider the specific circumstances.

Questions or corrections

If something appears inaccurate, confusing, or incorrectly attributed, contact prasad@indiananimeclub.com and include the relevant page URL and supporting information where possible.
