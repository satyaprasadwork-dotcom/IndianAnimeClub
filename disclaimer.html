Important information

An independent fan community.

Here is what readers should understand about the information and opinions published by Indian Anime Club.

Not an official anime organisation

Indian Anime Club is an independent fan community. Unless expressly disclosed and verifiable, we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any anime studio, manga publisher, distributor, streaming platform, event organiser, or rights holder.

Trademarks and copyrighted works

Anime and manga titles, characters, logos, artwork, music, and other third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners. References are made for identification, discussion, and fan-community context. Such references do not imply ownership or endorsement.

Opinions and accuracy

Commentary and recommendations represent the perspective of the relevant author and may be subjective. We aim to check factual statements and correct errors when identified, but cannot guarantee that every item is complete or current.

Events, partnerships, and availability

Unless a specific activity is clearly announced as confirmed, references to possible community activities or ideas should not be interpreted as bookings, ticket offers, partnerships, or commitments. No public event schedule is currently listed on this website.

External links

Links to other websites are offered as a convenience. We are not responsible for external content, policies, or services.
