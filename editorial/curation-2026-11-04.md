# Indian Anime Club — focused editorial curation, 4 November 2026

## Purpose and limits

Following a request to reduce potential Google AdSense low-value-content exposure, the 31 published article sources and the pre-existing claim review register were compared for distinct reader benefit, source-to-claim specificity, practical examples and headline support. This is an editorial judgement, **not** a finding that any page violates Google's policy, a certified plagiarism check, or proof of any change in AdSense acceptance probability. Google, not the publisher, determines site approval.

## Retired articles (4)

1. **How to Explain Why an Anime Scene Stayed With You** (`this-anime-scene-changed-how-fans-look-at-life`): largely teaches general scene-reading methods also covered more concretely by the retained writing/worldbuilding analysis guide. Consolidated into **Beyond Power Levels**.
2. **Anime in India: Language Access, Local Creativity and Community Opportunities** (`the-next-chapter-of-anime-in-india-language-access-local-creators-and-community-growth`): broad forward-looking discussion overlaps language, community and production-pipeline reporting, without independent evidence of outcomes. Consolidated toward **Could India Build a Bigger Animation-to-Anime Pipeline?**
3. **Food, Festivals and Family in Anime** (`festivals-food-and-family-exploring-everyday-cultural-details-in-anime-and-indian-life`): offers respectful comparison advice but few named anime-scene examples or distinct firsthand insight. Redirected to the retained India-specific family/viewing guide.
4. **How Anime Communities in Indian Cities Can Grow from Online Chats to Meetups** (`how-anime-communities-are-taking-shape-across-indian-cities-online-fandom-to-local-meetups`): describes general hypothetical meetup organisation instead of documenting named, verified city-level examples, overlapping the retained community leadership guide. Consolidated into **What Makes an Anime Community Last?**

The canonical source files and unused featured SVGs were removed. Archive and homepage cards, related links and sitemap entries were cleaned; Apache/LiteSpeed redirects preserve useful navigation. All 27 retained editorial articles preserve their original body copy, researched source links and author bylines. The full earlier review register remains in `editorial/review-2026-11-01.json` for historical accountability.

## Reasons for retaining the other 27

Each retained article has a distinct reader task or specific interpretive example, a visible source section, and over 1,000 reviewed body words under the publisher's own counting definition. This is *not* a Google word-count requirement. Particularly valuable resources cover legal access in India, language choices, reading order, beginner decisions, student budgets, cosplay, conventions, fan-art rights, creator skills, and group planning.

## Not verified

Independent external plagiarism comparisons, original image rights documentation, current Search Console index coverage, real visitor behaviour, platform permissions, regional consent/CMP settings and the private AdSense account's current status remain outside the source audit. No arbitrary 1% approval-risk figure should be attributed to any article.

## Layout changes (limited scope)

Article featured art maximum width increased from 640 to 960 CSS pixels on desktop, retaining the 16:9 ratio and responsive small-screen width. The official Google Preferred Sources control now appears within the existing author-information banner on every retained article, rather than at the left edge of the full-bleed article hero. Original theme, fonts, header, footer and article body layouts are unchanged.
