Privacy and data use

Know what is collected and why.

This notice describes the website's current use of analytics and the information we receive when someone contacts Indian Anime Club. Third-party services have their own data practices, described in their policies.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

At a glance

What this site uses

  • Google Analytics for website measurement
  • Microsoft Clarity for interaction insights
  • Google AdSense advertising code and related advertising technologies
  • Email and external social channels for direct messages

You do not need a website account to read our articles.

Privacy questions: satyaprasadwork@gmail.com

Who operates this website?

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused fan community founded by Satya Prasad. For questions about this notice or information you have sent us, contact satyaprasadwork@gmail.com.

Information you choose to send us

If you email us or contact us through Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, or another external service, we receive the details you choose to share. This can include your email address or platform username, message, links, and attachments. We use that information to respond, review feedback or correction requests, address concerns, and manage the conversation.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary. Social platforms handle your communication under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics with measurement ID G-M4738D1ZND to understand general website use. Google's default Analytics implementation can collect information such as user and session statistics, approximate location, browser and device information, and activity associated with a visit. Depending on configuration and visitor settings, Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers.

Read Google's explanation of Analytics data collection and the Google Privacy Policy for provider details.

Microsoft Clarity

The site also uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with pages and identify usability issues. Microsoft documents interaction and diagnostic events, such as clicks, scrolling, page visibility, and technical errors, along with information that can support playback-style analysis. The specific information available depends on the service and configuration. Do not enter sensitive personal information while browsing.

See Microsoft Clarity's data collection documentation and its privacy statement.

Cookies and available choices

Analytics services may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies for measurement. You can review your browser's privacy controls and the options available from service providers. Blocking scripts or cookies may reduce analytics functionality. The website does not currently offer a built-in analytics preference panel.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

This website includes Google AdSense advertising code. When Google ads are served or an advertising request is made, Google and other vendors may collect or receive information such as your IP address, browser and device details, cookie or similar identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and information used to deliver, measure, limit repetition of, and protect against invalid advertising activity. Depending on your settings, Google's and its partners' technologies may use information about visits to this site or other sites to personalise ads. Ads may also be non-personalised, depending on available settings, location, consent choices, and the advertising products used. The information processed and cookies used can vary with browser settings and Google's products.

Google explains its advertising data practices in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and its Privacy Policy. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also review industry opt-out choices at aboutads.info/choices. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising or all non-personalised measurement.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies in connection with ad serving. Other advertising vendors may participate depending on the ad settings and services enabled. Their own privacy notices and opt-out tools apply to their processing. The site does not currently provide a built-in cookie preference panel. Where consent or a certified consent-management solution is required for a visitor's region or the advertising configuration, the appropriate consent setup must be enabled before applicable advertising technologies are used.

External links

Article citations and community links may take you to external sites, including social networks, streaming services, publishers, or official information pages. We do not control those services, their availability, or privacy practices. Review their notices before using them.

Use, storage, and security

We use messages sent directly to us to respond and manage relevant editorial, community, or privacy requests. We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably useful for those purposes, record keeping, or issue resolution. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and young visitors

Indian Anime Club is intended for a general anime-fan audience. We do not ask visitors to submit personal information just to read articles. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us and you have a concern, contact us so we can review the request.

Questions or requests

You may ask about a message you sent or request deletion where appropriate. Tell us which communication or information you mean so we can locate it and avoid confusing it with someone else's message.

For background on India's data-protection framework, see the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules information. This page describes our current practices and is not advice for a specific legal situation.

Changes to this notice

If website features, analytics configuration, or information practices materially change, we will revise this page so readers can compare it with the current site. The date above identifies the latest revision.
