Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit Indian Anime Club or contact us.

Who operates this website

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-focused anime fan community founded by Satya Prasad. For privacy questions, contact prasad@indiananimeclub.com.

Information you choose to send

If you email us or contact us through social platforms, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, username, message, and any files you include. We use it to respond, review feedback, address concerns, and manage communication. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics with measurement ID G-M4738D1ZND to understand general site usage and improve the website. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical and usage information, such as pages visited, browser/device information, approximate location derived from technical data, and interactions. The exact information processed depends on Google's services and configuration.

For information about Google's handling of data, review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site functionality or measurement.

Cookies and advertising

At present, this policy does not claim that Indian Anime Club is serving third-party advertising. If advertising or other third-party services are added, those services may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies according to their own policies. We will update this notice to reflect material changes in data use. Third-party providers' data practices are governed by their own privacy notices.

External services and links

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp, Google, or other websites take you to services operated by others. We do not control their privacy practices, content, or security. Please review their terms and privacy notices before using them.

Retention and security

We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, resolve issues, or meet applicable obligations. We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary access to information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general anime-fan audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, please contact us.

Your choices and questions

You may choose not to contact us, adjust browser cookie settings, or use available privacy controls in third-party services. To ask about personal information you have sent us or to request its deletion where appropriate, email prasad@indiananimeclub.com.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when website features or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
