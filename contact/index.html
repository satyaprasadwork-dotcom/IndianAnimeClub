For corrections and article feedback

Send the exact article URL, the sentence or detail you are referring to, and a short explanation of what may be wrong. If possible, include a reliable source or primary document that supports the correction. For missing attribution or a copyright concern, identify the content and explain your connection to it without sending unnecessary personal documents.

For collaboration ideas

Describe the idea, who it is for, what you would like Indian Anime Club to do, and any relevant dates or links. Please be specific about whether you are proposing a community activity, an editorial contribution, an interview, or a partnership. A message is only a proposal; it does not mean an activity or partnership has been accepted or confirmed.

Response expectations

We review messages as capacity allows, but cannot promise a fixed response time or that every proposal will be accepted. Do not send passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through general contact channels.

Privacy: Information you choose to send in an email or third-party social platform is handled through that service. Read our Privacy Policy for details about website analytics and contact requests.
