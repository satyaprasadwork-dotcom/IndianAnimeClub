For anime fans across India

Anime is not a trend to us. It is part of our story.

Indian Anime Club is an independent, India-rooted community for people who love anime for its stories, craft, characters, music, and the memories it creates. No gatekeeping. No pretending. Just a more genuine space for fans.

Our point of view

More than a watchlist.

Anime means different things to different people. We want Indian fans to have space for all of it: the first series that hooked them, the story they still think about, the art they make, and the conversations that keep going after the credits.

01

Respect the medium

Talk about storytelling, animation, music, character development, and creative work—not just hype around the next release.

02

Rooted in India

Focus on the people, perspectives, fan culture, and community experiences that shape anime fandom across India.

03

Fans over noise

Build connections through honest discussion, shared interests, creativity, and respect—not inflated claims or empty promotion.

A community with a memory

We remember how it started.

For many Indian fans, anime was once discovered slowly: modem downloads, DVDs, borrowed recommendations, and sharing episodes with friends. Those early experiences still matter. They remind us that fandom is built by people, not just platforms.

Indian Anime Club was founded by Satya Prasad, an anime fan with more than 20 years of watching experience. The club's goal is to make room for thoughtful conversations and a welcoming community for fans in India.
