Community standards

Different favourites. Shared respect.

Anime fandom is better when people can disagree about stories without attacking one another. These guidelines apply to participation in spaces operated by Indian Anime Club.

Be respectful

Discuss series, characters, creators, and opinions without harassment, threats, hate, or personal attacks. Disagreement is welcome; targeting people is not.

Respect spoilers

Use clear spoiler warnings when discussing major plot developments. Give other fans a reasonable chance to experience a story for themselves.

Credit creators and rights holders

Do not claim another person's artwork, writing, cosplay photography, or other work as your own. Ask permission where appropriate, credit the creator, and respect requests to remove content. Anime, manga, music, characters, and official artwork remain the property of their respective rights holders.

No spam or misleading promotion

Do not flood community spaces with repetitive posts, deceptive links, scams, impersonation, or undisclosed commercial promotion. If you have a genuine collaboration proposal, contact us directly.

Protect privacy and safety

Do not share another person's private information, screenshots of private conversations, or identifying details without permission. Do not pressure people to reveal personal details or move conversations off-platform.

Keep the community welcoming

New fans, long-time fans, sub watchers, dub watchers, casual viewers, collectors, artists, and cosplayers should all be able to participate. Do not use someone's experience level or favourite series as an excuse to belittle them.

Moderation

Indian Anime Club may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. We may update these guidelines as the community grows. If you encounter a concern, contact prasad@indiananimeclub.com.

Participation in third-party platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp is also subject to those platforms' own terms, privacy settings, and moderation tools.
