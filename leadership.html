Leadership

Built by a fan who remembers the journey.

Indian Anime Club starts with a simple belief: the community deserves to be built by people who understand what anime has meant to fans over time.

SP
Founder

Satya Prasad

Founder, Indian Anime Club

I have been watching anime for more than 20 years. When I was a kid, finding and sharing anime took effort: I remember downloading episodes over modem connections, watching DVDs, and sharing what I found with friends. Discovering a new series felt personal because it often came through curiosity, patience, and someone else's recommendation.

That experience shaped how I see fandom. Anime is not just a label to put on a page or a trend to chase. It is storytelling, animation, music, imagination, and memories people carry for years. I founded Indian Anime Club to help create a more genuine community for anime fans in India—one that respects the medium and the people who love it.

The goal is not to pretend that we already have a large organisation or a calendar full of activities. The goal is to build carefully, listen to fans, share useful and original perspectives, and grow a community that earns trust over time.

Leadership principles

  • Fan-first: community value comes before hype, sales, or vanity metrics.
  • Honesty: no invented events, inflated claims, or implied partnerships.
  • Respect for creators: credit artists and rights holders; do not present fan content as official material.
  • India-rooted: keep the community's perspective grounded in anime fandom in India and the people shaping it.
  • Build in public: explain what the club is, what it is not, and how people can contribute.

Indian Anime Club currently identifies one founder: Satya Prasad. Additional leadership roles will be listed only when people have formally joined and agreed to be represented here.
